Academic Activity Tracker & Ledger

A spreadsheet system that tracks academic activities, deadlines, and submissions, automated with Apps Script.

Overview

A personal tracking system that organizes academic activities, monitors deadlines, and keeps submission records in one spreadsheet enhanced with Google Apps Script to automate repetitive tracking, document organization, and record synchronization.

Objectives

  • Keep academic activities, deadlines, and submission records in one place
  • Monitor deadlines and submission status at a glance
  • Automate repetitive tracking and document organization

The challenge

Academic activities, attendance proofs, supporting files, and class notes can end up spread across many sheets and Drive folders, which makes deadlines and submissions easy to lose track of.

Approach and process

  1. Organized activity records with deadline monitoring and a remaining-days calculation
  2. Tracked submission status and added an activity summary with unique activity identification
  3. Used Google Apps Script to match attendance IDs to files in Google Drive and create proof links
  4. Linked supporting files to their respective submission records
  5. Synchronized class notes and related records across sheets and Drive folders
